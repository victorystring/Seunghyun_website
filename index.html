About

Researcher in computational mechanics and multiphysics simulations

I am a researcher working on advanced multiphysics simulations involving fluid dynamics, plasma physics, heat transfer, and electromagnetic coupling phenomena.

My research aims to develop high-fidelity numerical frameworks capable of predicting complex industrial processes such as electric furnaces, thermal plasma systems, and advanced manufacturing technologies.

I am also interested in integrating physics-based models with machine learning approaches to accelerate simulations while maintaining physical consistency and predictive capability.

Research Areas

Current and emerging research topics

Multiphase Flow

Numerical modeling of gas-liquid-solid interactions, granular flow, and industrial fluidized systems.

Thermal Plasma

Simulation of arc plasma, plasma jets, and electromagnetically coupled thermal systems.

Magnetohydrodynamics

Investigation of electromagnetic-fluid coupling and conductive flow phenomena.

Scientific Machine Learning

Integration of AI and physics-based simulations for accelerated predictive modeling.

Advanced Manufacturing

Modeling of atomization, thermal spray, additive manufacturing, and material processing.

Computational Engineering

Development of high-performance computational frameworks for industrial-scale simulations.
