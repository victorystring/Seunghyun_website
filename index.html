Papers & Patents

2026.06 - Journal Article

Numerical investigation of cold fluidization behavior using a Dual-Size Eulerian Model (DSEM): Effects of particle size on hydrodynamics in fluidized bed reactors

Seunghyun Sim, Bong-Min Jin and Hyunjin Yang

Developed a Dual-Size Eulerian Model (DSEM) to simulate cold fluidized beds with a wide particle size distribution using two representative particle sizes. This work focused on analyzing particle size effects in hydrogen-based fluidized beds and successfully captured segregation and entrainment phenomena in gas-solid flows.

Research Keywords

Hydrogen-based fluidized bed, Segregation, Entrainment, Computational fluid dynamics (CFD), Representative particle size

Main Contributions
- Proposed the Dual-Size Eulerian Model (DSEM) framework
- Developed and validated the DSEM
- Analyzed segregation/entrainment behavior using a fluidization regime map
- Suggested representative particle size strategies for industrial applications

2025.09 - Journal Article

A Practical 1D Approach for Real-Time Prediction of Argon Flow and Pressure in Continuous Casting of Steel

Hyunjin Yang, Bong-Min Jin, Hyeonjin Kim, Seungwon Seo and Seunghyun Sim

Developed a one-dimensional (1D) analytical model for compressible argon flow in stopper rods during continuous steel casting, incorporating gas dynamics and heat transfer. The model was validated through pilot-scale experiments and CFD simulations, and demonstrated real-time prediction capabilities for air aspiration, argon leakage, and gas injection behavior in SEN systems.

Research Keywords

thermal expansion; compressible flow; refractory; air aspiration; continuous caster simulator (CCS)

Main Contributions
- Validated the 1D model through CFD simulations
- Post-processing and visualization
