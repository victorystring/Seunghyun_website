Papers & Patents

2026.06 - Journal Article

Numerical investigation of cold fluidization behavior using a Dual-Size Eulerian Model (DSEM): Effects of particle size on hydrodynamics in fluidized bed reactors

Seunghyun Sim, Bong-Min Jin and Hyunjin Yang

Developed a Dual-Size Eulerian Model (DSEM) to simulate cold fluidized beds with a wide particle size distribution using two representative particle sizes. This work focused on analyzing particle size effects in hydrogen-based fluidized beds and successfully captured segregation and entrainment phenomena in gas-solid flows.

Research Keywords

Hydrogen-based fluidized bed, Segregation, Entrainment, Computational fluid dynamics (CFD), Representative particle size

Main Contributions
- Proposed the Dual-Size Eulerian Model (DSEM) framework
- Developed and validated the DSEM
- Analyzed segregation/entrainment behavior using a fluidization regime map
- Suggested representative particle size strategies for industrial applications

2025.09 - Journal Article

A Practical 1D Approach for Real-Time Prediction of Argon Flow and Pressure in Continuous Casting of Steel

Hyunjin Yang, Bong-Min Jin, Hyeonjin Kim, Seungwon Seo and Seunghyun Sim

Developed a one-dimensional (1D) analytical model for compressible argon flow in stopper rods during continuous steel casting, incorporating gas dynamics and heat transfer. The model was validated through pilot-scale experiments and CFD simulations, and demonstrated real-time prediction capabilities for air aspiration, argon leakage, and gas injection behavior in SEN systems.

Research Keywords

thermal expansion; compressible flow; refractory; air aspiration; continuous caster simulator (CCS)

Main Contributions
- Validated the 1D model through CFD simulations
- Post-processing and visualization

Method and System for Predicting Argon Gas Line Pressure and Flow Rate in Continuous Casting Process

Hyunjin Yang, Bong-Min Jin, Hyeonjin Kim, Seungwon Seo and Seunghyun Sim

2025 - Patent (In process)

Stopper Nozzle for Continuous Casting

Hyunjin Yang, Bong-Min Jin, Hyeonjin Kim, Seungwon Seo and Seunghyun Sim

Designed the outlet nozzle diameter of the argon line beneath the stopper to induce choked flow, thereby preventing negative pressure generated downstream inside the SEN from propagating upstream to the argon line. This design also prevented external air ingress through the argon line and maintained a constant mass flow rate under choked conditions, avoiding excessive argon gas injection.

Research Keywords

Continuous steel casting, Argon injection nozzle, Air aspiration, Choked flow

Main Contributions
- Conducted CFD analysis to evaluate pressure distribution in the argon line.

2024 - Patent

Automatic Current Waveform Generation Program for Tactile and Perceived Quality Control of Electronic Dials

H. Yang, S. Seo, J. Kim, S. Sim, J. Son, J. Park

Conducted research on enhancing the tactile feel of a dial composed of magnetorheological (MR) fluid whose viscosity varies with the applied current. After developing a physics-based model to calculate the torque generated by the applied current, a deep learning model was trained to generate current waveforms from target torque profiles. Using this model, current waveforms corresponding to torque profiles perceived as premium by users were identified.

Research Keywords

Magnetorheological fluid, Bingham plastic model, deep learning, Fourier transform, MPLAB

Main Contributions
- Developed a MATLAB code for torque calculation based on the Bingham plastic model.
- Proposed a Fourier transform–based approach for torque signal analysis.
